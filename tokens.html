D-TOK

Twenty literal colours, three decisions.

Everything else in the sweep is mechanical. These three need eyes, because each one either adds a token to the ramp or moves something on screen. Nothing here is applied to the site yet.

1. Body copy

p { color: ? }

Today it is #5c5c5c at 6.69:1 — a fifth neutral that is not on the ramp, and lands exactly halfway between --ink-2 and --ink-3. Read a full sample, not a swatch. Use Solo to see them one at a time; side by side, the lighter grey always looks lighter than it really is.

2. Near-white surfaces

4 fills within 1.04–1.33:1

A figure background, the wireframe bars, the progress rail and the ghost button’s border. All four are almost paper. The question is whether they are one surface or a scale — look at the wireframe bars against the figure behind them, since that is the only pairing where the steps do any work.
