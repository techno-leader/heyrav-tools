Sparse scatter, contact in the hole

Ten assets across fifteen tiles — five appear twice, never next to their own copy, which is what makes a small set read as a field instead of a gallery. At rest the field is grey, per-image graded to one common exposure and carrying a film grain, and the void holds the contact pitch. Hover any tile and its group lights: the line in the void becomes that project's sentence, the buttons step out, and every tile in the group comes back into colour at once — grain and all. Nothing moves or scales.

Two things changed that weren't asked for, flagged rather than buried: tile alt text is now written out instead of being the raw file name (the old one shipped onbaording and shayog to screen readers), and the Ashwaresin / Shilajit pair is captioned as one “photoshoots” group, so neither product is named on screen any more.
