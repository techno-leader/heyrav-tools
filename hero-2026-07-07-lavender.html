03 — Who I Am

I sit where design meets code meets revenue. Two years in, I've shipped work across the full stack of persuasion — pixel-level interface craft, motion that guides attention, and the funnel math that tells you whether any of it worked.

Most designers stop at the mockup. I prototype in code, ship the real thing, then instrument it — because a design isn't finished when it looks right, it's finished when the number moves.

Currently open to product design and design-engineering roles with teams that care about craft and outcomes in equal measure.
