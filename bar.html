D-BAR

Scroll progress bar, five tails.

The bar that ships today is a flat 2px fill in --ink-3 with a hard right edge. “Faded tail” has two readings — the trailing end can dissolve, or the leading edge can. Both are below. Drag the slider to park every bar at the same position, or turn on the sweep to watch them move together.

Tail length is one token, --tail, shared by C, D and E — the slider above retunes all three at once. Whichever you pick becomes four lines in each case page, replacing .progress{…background:var(--ink-3)}.
